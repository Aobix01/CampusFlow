The problem

Deadlines, tests, club meetings and events live in five different places. Students miss things, not because they're lazy, but because nothing is in one place.

What CampusFlow does

🧭

Smart Planning

Smart Priority ranks your tasks by deadline and importance. Plan My Day fits them into the hours you have.

✅

Task Management

Assignments, exams, clubs, events and personal tasks together. Search, filter, sort.

🤝

Friend Accountability

See friends' streaks and completion rates, never their private tasks.

🏆

Weekly Leaderboards

Ranked by completion %, not task count, so a light week can still win.

How it works

  1. Dump everything in. Add tasks one by one, or type a messy sentence into the AI Planning Prototype.
  2. Follow the plan. Smart Priority shows what to do first; Plan My Day builds a schedule.
  3. Compete gently. Finish what you planned, climb Weekly Flow, win the canteen round.
